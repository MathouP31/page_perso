## Artishow Project

[Artishow](https://artishow.r2.enst.fr/) is a multidisciplinary collaborative project at Télécom Paris where ~240 first-year students work in teams of 4–5 to design and build an original project combining at least two technical disciplines. The semester culminates in a public exhibition on campus. As a supervisor, I guided student teams through the research, design, and implementation phases.

- **2024** — Project supervisor, Télécom Paris
- **2026** — Project supervisor, Télécom Paris
