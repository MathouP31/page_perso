## Artishow Project

[Artishow](https://artishow.r2.enst.fr/) is a multidisciplinary collaborative project at Télécom Paris where first-year students work in teams of 4–5 to design and build an original project. The semester culminates in a public exhibition on campus. As a supervisor, I guided student teams through the research, design, and implementation phases.

- **2024** — Building and Exploring a Scientific Collaboration Graph: scraping the HAL platform to build a collaboration graph and running fairness analyses on the sensitive attribute of gender.
- **2026** — Bot detection on the [2024 U.S. Presidential Election Twitter/X dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00376) to study the influence of bots on the 2024 U.S. election.
